Priya owns the shape of Harbor's request path. She wrote
[ADR-0004](../decisions/0004-session-state-in-redis.md) in 2025 to get sticky
sessions out of the load balancer, and wrote
[ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-session-state-in-signed-cookies.md) eighteen months
later to undo it.

She is the person to ask why a thing is the way it is, which is exactly the
knowledge this repository exists to stop being a person.
